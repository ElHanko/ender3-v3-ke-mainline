# Acknowledgements

Fre3nder builds on publicly available open-source software, documentation,
hardware research, and reverse-engineering work from the wider community.

## NebulaOS / OpenKE

The NebulaOS and OpenKE projects provide valuable public reference material
for the Creality Ender-3 V3 KE and Ingenic X2000 platform.

Their published work includes board-level hardware research, comparisons
against stock firmware and device trees, and real-hardware qualification.
This has helped Fre3nder avoid repeating already documented hardware
investigation from scratch.

Fre3nder treats external findings as reference evidence rather than as
authority. Applicable findings are checked against Fre3nder's pinned kernel,
drivers, device tree, and observed hardware before they are adopted.

Repositories:

- https://github.com/coreflake1/NebulaOS
- https://github.com/coreflake1/NebulaOS-firmware
- https://github.com/coreflake1/NebulaOS-kernel
