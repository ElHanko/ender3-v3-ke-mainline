# Contributing to Fre3nder

By submitting a contribution, you confirm that you have the rights necessary
to submit it under the applicable license.

- Contributions to the productive Fre3nder system are submitted under
  `AGPL-3.0-or-later`.
- Project-authored contributions to `research/` are submitted under `MIT`,
  unless the file or work is already subject to another license.
- Third-party and derived works must retain their applicable license,
  copyright notices, and provenance.

No contributor license agreement, copyright assignment, or separate commercial
license is required.
